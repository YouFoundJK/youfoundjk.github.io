### Mathematical Focus
Exploration of phase space as a smooth manifold endowed with a closed, non-degenerate 2-form $\omega$ (the symplectic form).

### Covered Topics
- **Symplectic Manifolds & Hamiltonian Vector Fields**: Conservation of energy via Darboux's theorem and Poisson brackets.
- **Lie Groups & Moment Maps**: Noether's theorem formulated through momentum maps and symplectic reduction.
- **Geometric Quantization**: Bridging classical phase space $(M, \omega)$ to quantum Hilbert spaces $\mathcal{H}$ via prequantization line bundles and polarizations.
